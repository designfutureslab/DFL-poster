Follow these steps to load filament into the Prusa MK4S.

> [!IMPORTANT] Heat first
> Allow the printer to fully heat before inserting filament. Forcing cold filament will jam the extruder.

## Preheat the Printer

[QR: Watch this step](https://example.com/preheat-video)

On the LCD panel, navigate to **Temperature → Preheat** and select your material:

- **PLA** → 215 °C nozzle / 60 °C bed
- **PETG** → 230 °C nozzle / 85 °C bed

Wait for the nozzle to reach the target temperature before continuing.

## Cut and Prepare the Filament

Cut a clean, straight end at a 45° angle using the side-cutters by the filament rack.

> [!TIP] Curled end?
> Cut back 20–30 mm until you have a clean tip.

## Insert Filament into the Extruder

1. Hold the spool so it unwinds counter-clockwise
2. Guide the tip into the PTFE tube at the top of the extruder
3. Push gently until you feel resistance

Then select **Filament → Load Filament** and confirm.

## Confirm Extrusion

Watch the nozzle tip. The printer purges roughly 30 mm of filament.

> [!NOTE] Colour changes
> Going from dark to light colours may need 2–3 purge cycles.
