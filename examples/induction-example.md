## Overview

The Prusa MK4S is a desktop FDM printer for PLA, PETG, ASA and flexible materials. Build volume is **250 × 210 × 220 mm**.

You must complete this induction before using the printer unsupervised.

## Safety Requirements

[QR: Safety walkthrough video](https://example.com/videos/prusa-safety)

> [!DANGER] Fire risk
> Never leave the printer unattended during the first layer. Adhesion failures can pile filament on the nozzle.

> [!WARNING] Hot surfaces
> The hot end reaches 290 °C and the bed 120 °C. Allow 5 minutes to cool before touching either.

> [!IMPORTANT] Smell burning?
> Cancel the print immediately and tell a lab technician.

### PPE

- Safety glasses when loading or unloading filament
- Heat-resistant gloves when removing prints from a hot bed

## Starting a Print

1. Slice your model in PrusaSlicer using the correct printer and filament profile
2. Export to USB or send via PrusaConnect
3. Select your file from **LCD → Print**
4. Stay for the first 3–4 minutes and confirm the first layer is adhering
5. Log the print in the booking system before leaving

> [!NOTE] Logging
> Prints that are not logged cannot be traced if there is an incident.

## Emergency Procedure

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Fire / smoke | Cancel print, hit E-STOP, evacuate, pull fire alarm |
| Filament jam | Cancel print, let cool, follow the jam guide |
| Electrical fault | Do not touch. Isolate at the wall socket, notify technician |
