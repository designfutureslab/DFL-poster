## Before you start

> [!WARNING] Hot end
> Heat the nozzle to 170 °C to soften the filament, then **switch the printer off at the wall** before touching anything.

## Steps

1. Unload the filament from the LCD menu
2. Hold the heater block with the wrench and unscrew the old nozzle
3. Thread the new nozzle in by hand, then nip it up a quarter turn
4. Power on and run **Calibration → First Layer**

## Troubleshooting

[QR: Jam-clearing guide](https://example.com/jam-guide)

> [!TIP] Leaking at the thread?
> Re-tighten while the hot end is at 170 °C. It is much easier to seal hot than cold.
