## Before You Enter

- Safety glasses must be worn at all times
- Closed-toe shoes are mandatory
- Tie back long hair before operating any machine
- No loose clothing near moving parts

> [!WARNING] Hot surfaces
> Nozzle temperatures can exceed 250 °C. Never touch the nozzle or bed without a cool-down period.
